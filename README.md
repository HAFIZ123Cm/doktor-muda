# Doktor Muda

A free clinic-simulator and exam-practice game for medical students: take a history in your own words (English or Malay), examine, order investigations, diagnose and manage — then get examiner-style feedback. Includes a story career, semester-exam practice by posting, and Pro-exam formats (SBA, SBQ, OSCE, long case, short case).

**Play:** https://hafiz123cm.github.io/doktor-muda/ (works on phones, tablets and computers — landscape is best; "Add to Home Screen" to install).

- Saves stay in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
- Optional AI patient mode uses your own Claude API key, stored only in your browser.
- Educational study game, not medical advice. Fictional patients and clinic. Not affiliated with any university or the Ministry of Health.

Built with Godot Engine and Dialogic. See THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt.
